Join the net

Get your ham radio license

You don't need a license to watch this page — you need one to check in on the air and report what you see.

Why bother

A Technician-class license is the entry ticket to Skywarn net participation on 2 meters and 70 centimeters — no Morse code has been required since 2007, and the exam is 35 multiple-choice questions pulled from a public question pool. Most first-time candidates pass after a weekend of focused study.

General and Extra class open up HF privileges used elsewhere in Arkansas AUXCOMM operations — useful once you're active locally, but not a prerequisite for your first net check-in.
